001

Her Presence

The quiet kind of magic.

I don't know when I started noticing it.

Maybe it was somewhere between the ordinary conversations and the moments that stayed with me longer than they should have.

There is something about her presence that doesn't ask to be noticed.

And somehow,
I notice it anyway.

She doesn't have to say much.
She doesn't have to stay long.

Somehow, even the smallest part of her day reaching mine is enough to make mine feel a little different.

I think that's what I mean when I say she has a presence.

Not something loud.
Not something she tries to create.

Just her.

And somehow, that's enough.

- Hyunoo

003

through my eyes

The Kind of Person
She Is

The parts of her that make her, her.

03

some people are difficult to describe
because a few words could never be enough.

I don't think it's one particular thing that makes me admire her.

It's the way all these different pieces somehow exist in the same person.

01 / 05

Kind.

Not in a way that asks to be noticed. I think that's what makes it beautiful.

Sometimes kindness is simply the way someone makes the world around them feel a little softer.

02 / 05

Intelligent.

And not simply because she knows things. I admire the way her mind works.

The way she thinks. The way she notices. The way a conversation with her can leave me thinking long after it ends.

03 / 05

Independent.

There is something about the way she carries her own life that I deeply respect.

She has her own world, her own rhythm, her own way of finding her way through things.

04 / 05

Complicated.

Not complicated in a way I want to solve.

More like a book I don't want to skip ahead in. I'd rather discover her slowly, page by page, for as long as she'll let me.

05 / 05

Funny.

Sometimes intentionally. Sometimes probably without realizing it.

Either way, she has a habit of leaving a smile behind.

✦

And still, those words don't quite do it.

I could probably keep finding words for her.

Kind. Intelligent. Independent.
Complicated. Funny.

But I don't think any collection of adjectives could ever explain a person completely.

She is simply
herself.

And maybe that's the part
I admire most.

- Hyunoo

004

A Life That Feels Like Home

I hope life becomes gentle with you.

Not perfect.
Not without difficult days.

Just gentle enough that you never have to question whether the good days are coming.

01

I WISH YOU

Home.

I hope you wake up in places that feel like home. I hope there is always coffee the way you like it, sunlight through your windows, and something waiting for you that makes you excited to begin the day.

02

I WISH YOU

Good people.

People who make staying feel easy.

People who listen when you speak.
Who remember the little things.
Who make room for every version of you,
even the quiet ones.

03

I WISH YOU

Love.

I hope you are loved without having to earn it.

not because of what you can give. not because of how strong you can be. not because you always know what to say.

Just because
you are you.

04

I WISH YOU

A full life.

I hope you keep finding reasons to laugh until your stomach hurts.

Trips you haven't planned yet.
Songs that become part of your life.
Places you never knew you would love.
Ordinary Tuesdays that somehow become memories worth keeping.

05

I WISH YOU

Room to be yourself.

I hope you never have to make yourself smaller to fit inside someone else's world.

Stay curious.
A little stubborn.
A little complicated.
Completely yourself.

✦

I hope the things you quietly dream about find their way to you.

Even the ones you've never told anyone.

06

AND WHEN LIFE IS HEAVY

Be gentle
with yourself.

I hope you remember how many difficult days you've already made it through.

And I hope you give yourself the same patience and kindness you so easily give to others.

Most of all,

I hope you build
a life you don't
need to escape from.

A life that feels warm when you come home to it.
A life filled with people you love.
A life where you are safe, understood,
challenged, surprised, and deeply happy.

And maybe there will be parts of that life I never get to see.

places I won't be there for. stories I won't hear. versions of you I may never get to know.

That's okay.

Because when I say I want good things for you,

I don't mean only
the good things
that include me.

✦

I mean all of them.

Every beautiful thing this life
can possibly give you.

And wherever life takes you,

I hope you look around one day and realize:

this is the life
I once hoped for.

And I hope you smile.

Because you made it there.

You deserve to.

- Hyunoo

005

Us, Someday

not a promise. just a thought I like keeping.

005

maybe, somewhere, someday.

I don't know what will happen.

But sometimes,
I let myself imagine.

Not forever.
Not some perfect story.
Nothing that life has promised us.

Just you and me,
somewhere distance doesn't matter anymore.

01

I THINK ABOUT

The ordinary things.

Sitting across from you instead of looking at your name on a screen.

Coffee getting cold because the conversation lasted longer than either of us noticed.

Walking somewhere without needing a reason to be going anywhere at all.

Maybe that's what I want most.

To exist in the
same place as you.

Even if only for a little while.

02

BUT I KNOW

Life owes us nothing.

I don't know where we'll be years from now. I don't know what life will change, or who we'll become along the way.

So I won't turn this feeling into a future that hasn't happened.

Still...

I like knowing
the possibility exists.

That somewhere in all the versions of tomorrow,

there might be one
where we finally meet.

And if that day ever comes,

I hope it feels strangely ordinary.

Like we've done it a hundred times before.

Like distance was only something we had to cross to get there.

✦

Until then,

I'll leave us here.
Somewhere between
maybe and someday.
