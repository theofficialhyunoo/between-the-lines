001 AUGUST 2026

Somewhere Between Our Conversations

I don't really know when it happened.

I don’t know exactly when it happened. There wasn’t one conversation I could point to and say, *there, that was the moment.* I think it happened somewhere in between.

Between the random conversations, the little jokes, the stories about our days, and all those ordinary moments that probably seemed small at the time. Somewhere between all of that, I started looking forward to you.

I started remembering the little things you told me. I noticed how easily you could make me smile. Your name appearing on my screen became one of those small things that could quietly make my day better. And I liked that.

I liked getting to know you slowly.

Not the version of you I could imagine in my head, but the real pieces of yourself you chose to share with me. Your thoughts, your humor, your stories, your good days, your complicated ones.

Somewhere along the way, I realized that you had become special to me.

I never needed that realization to become a question you had to answer. These feelings are mine, and I’m happy to carry them gently. Because knowing you reminded me that sometimes someone can enter your life without any grand beginning and still leave something beautiful behind.

You did that for me.

You made an ordinary part of my life feel a little less ordinary.

And maybe that’s all I wanted to write down.

Not where this goes.

Not what it means to you.

Not what could have been.

Just what was true for me:

Somewhere between our conversations, without meaning to and without asking anything from you, I grew very fond of you. And I’m glad I did.

- Hyunoo

002 SEPTEMBER 2026

If Only You Knew

If only you knew how many times I have almost told you.

How many messages I have written and erased. How many times I have wanted to say something honest, only to replace it with something smaller, safer, easier to carry.

Sometimes I want to tell you that I miss you.

Not in some dramatic way. Just in the quiet way someone begins to notice the absence of a person who has somehow become part of their day.

I want to tell you that I look forward to hearing from you more than I probably should. That sometimes your name appearing on my screen is enough to change the mood of an entire day.

I want to tell you that I notice you.

The little things you say. The stories you tell me. The things that make you laugh. The pieces of yourself you probably don't even realize you've left in my memory.

And sometimes, I wonder if you know.

Maybe you do.

Maybe somewhere between all the things I say and all the things I carefully don't, you've already figured me out.

But there is one thing I don't think I have ever managed to say properly.

I don't need anything from you for these feelings to have been worth having.

I don't need a promise. I don't need an answer. I don't need to know where any of this goes.

I just wish, sometimes, that you could see yourself through my eyes.

Maybe then you would understand why it became so difficult to keep everything between the lines.

And if you ever wondered whether there was something I wasn't telling you...

There was.

There still is.

– Hyunoo

003 UNDATED

In Another Distance

Sometimes I wonder what would have happened if the world had placed us a little closer.

Not in another life. Not in some impossible universe. Just here, in this one, with a little less distance between us.

I wonder what it would be like if talking to you didn't require looking at the time in another country.

If good morning actually meant we were waking beneath the same morning.

If seeing you didn't have to begin with airports, passports, and thousands of miles.

Maybe we would have met for coffee somewhere.

Maybe I would have learned the sound of your laugh without a screen between us.

Maybe there would be ordinary things.

Walking somewhere with no destination. Sitting across from each other over dinner. Complaining about work. Sharing food. Being quiet without needing to fill the silence.

Nothing extraordinary.

And somehow, that's the part I think about most.

Because when someone is this far away, even ordinary moments begin to feel like impossible little luxuries.

I don't know whether being closer would have changed anything between us.

Maybe it wouldn't have.

Maybe we would still be exactly what we are now.

But sometimes I let myself wonder.

Not about some grand future. Not about promises we never made.

Just about a version of this world where reaching you was a little easier.

Where I could say, "Do you want to get dinner?" instead of wondering how many hours separate your evening from mine.

Maybe that's what distance does.

It makes you long for things that other people would consider ordinary.

A coffee. A walk. A conversation that doesn't end because one of us has to sleep.

Just once, being under the same sky and not having it feel quite so enormous.

– Hyunoo
