001

I Missed You Today

I missed you today.

Not in some dramatic, unbearable way. I just kept having little moments where I wanted to tell you something.

And somewhere between those moments, I realized your absence had become something I could notice.

I almost told you.

I decided to keep it here instead.

002

I Wait For Your Name

Sometimes I wait for your name.

I don't mean that I sit there staring at my screen, waiting for you to appear.

I still go about my day. I work. I laugh. I get distracted. Life continues exactly as it should.

But every now and then, my phone lights up, and for that tiny second before I look, I hope it's you.

I don't think you know that.

Maybe you never will.
