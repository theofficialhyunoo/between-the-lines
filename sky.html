01

THE HOURS WE SHARE

Somehow,
our clocks agree.

It's morning where you are and night where I am.

Two completely different parts of the day, happening at exactly the same time.

And because of work, our lives overlap there.

Your daytime became part of my nights.

Different countries. Different time zones.
The same team. The same hours.

02

A SMALL HABIT

Sometimes I send you the sky.

Not because there's anything extraordinary about it.

a sunset. a cloud shaped strangely. the moon looking particularly beautiful. a color I thought you might like.
✦

But somewhere along the way, showing you the sky became another way of saying:

I thought
of you.

without actually having to say it.

03

I SOMETIMES WONDER

Have we ever looked up at the same time?

Maybe.

We're awake together more often than the distance would make anyone think.

Maybe there has been a moment when you looked outside during your day while I looked outside during my night.

Two different skies in front of us.
One world above us.

Different countries.

Different hours.

Different horizons.

The same sky
above us.

On a map, it looks enormous.

oceans. countries. continents.

Enough distance to make two people feel like they belong to completely different worlds.

✦

But the sky has no borders drawn across it.

It doesn't know where one country ends and another begins.

Maybe that's why I like looking at it.

For a moment,
the world feels smaller.

And you don't feel
quite so far away.

MAYBE

Someday.

I won't have to photograph the sky and send it to you.

Maybe you'll simply be standing beside me. No twelve-hour difference to think about. No screen between us. No wondering what the sky looks like where you are.

Just one place.

One evening.

And the same sky
above both of us.

I don't know if life will ever give us that moment.

But if it does,

I think I'll remember all the nights I looked up from this side of the world and wondered what the sky looked like from yours.

Until then,

when something beautiful appears above me,

a part of me will probably still wonder

if you can
see it too.
