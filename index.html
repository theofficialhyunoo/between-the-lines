I don't know if that universe exists.

Maybe somewhere, there is a version of us who never had to wonder what this could have been.

Maybe they got the timing right.

Maybe they got the distance right.

Maybe they were given the ordinary days that we could only imagine.

But I'm grateful for this universe too.

Because out of all the lives I could have lived, all the people I could have met, and all the ways our paths could have missed each other completely, somehow mine still found yours.

Even from this far away.

And maybe that's enough for me to be grateful that this universe gave me you at all.
